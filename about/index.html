About ManaScout

ManaScout is an independent Magic: The Gathering publisher and card research tool. It helps you tell similar cards apart, compare artwork and finishes, and record the printing you intend to use in Deck Studio.

How we research printings

ManaScout publishes and reviews the guides. We start with Scryfall catalogue records for printing identities, set codes, collector numbers, languages, artists and available finishes. Guides link to relevant records and cite Wizards of the Coast product or rules material and other catalogue sources where they support the claim.

To compare cards, check the set and collector number together, then confirm the artwork, language and finish. Some older cards have catalogue numbers that do not appear on the physical card. Where sources disagree or a treatment is uncertain, the guide explains the distinction and links to the evidence available.

Research and review dates describe the work recorded in the guide. Live catalogue data in the explorer can change independently of that editorial review.

Corrections

If a guide or catalogue match looks wrong, report it through Contact. Include the guide URL, card name, set code, collector number, language and finish, with a catalogue or official source when available. Those details let us check the specific printing and correct the guide where warranted.

Retailer links and affiliate disclosure

ManaScout provides retailer links for finding listings. A retailer name search can return several editions. Confirm the set, number, language, condition and finish with the seller before buying. The retailer controls prices, availability, shipping and order support.

Where a link is identified as an affiliate link, ManaScout may earn a commission from a qualifying purchase. In programmes that pay commission without adding a charge to the order, following the link does not increase your purchase price. Affiliate tracking is currently inactive.

Commercial relationships do not determine how ManaScout identifies a card or which sources support a guide. Scryfall links are for catalogue verification. ManaScout is independent of Wizards of the Coast, Scryfall and the retailers it links to.
