## Internal opportunity process

No public SEO score or projected click count is added. The process is a short triage, not a predictive formula:

1. Exclude the five Phase 5 title/description/opening experiments before ranking opportunities.
2. Review position 2–10 pages with zero/low clicks and at least the supplied 10 impressions. Prioritise meaningful exposure and a concrete collector question, not position alone. Fierce Guardianship at ~3.20 has only ten impressions: high rank is useful, but that small sample cannot establish a persistent CTR defect.
3. Include a smaller near-page-one group (~10–13) when its printing/art proposition can be sharpened. Better wording does not substitute for better ranking; inspect position/query/device movement at review.
4. Keep Black Lotus separate at ~25. Its inclusion is a long-tail edition/original-art clarification, not a claim that the generic `black lotus` query is a near-term opportunity. Its page explicitly disclaims authenticity and valuation conclusions.
5. Record the actual prior wording and change only what improves identification or route clarity. Preserve research tables, source dates, legality caveats and detailed art/finish history.
