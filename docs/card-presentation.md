## Featured surface and atmosphere

All Minas Morgul atmospheric styles now live in `home.css`. The inline animation block and stacked older override blocks were removed. Exact set/collector-number/card-name selectors scope the feature to LTC #390; a future featured card does not inherit its surface treatment. Homepage editorial styling was retained when removing the inline style block.

The outer `weekly-card-float` surface now supplies a continuous 13-second floating/tilting cycle on desktop and mobile. The inner shell supplies pointer/touch tilt on both axes, up to 5° X and 6° Y, returning over 380ms. Idle movement pauses at its current angle during interaction and resumes on release; nested surfaces keep their transforms independent. Curved foil fields move independently over 9/14-second cycles, with an occasional curved 19-second highlight. Pointer position adjusts reflection opacity within 0.48–0.84 (idle 0.68) and moves the reflection surface. Multiple green, silver and muted spectral bands replace the barely perceptible 0.26 × low-alpha reflections. No saturation/brightness filter changes the authentic artwork. 5G replaces moving fog/bats with a card-anchored spectral halo and static black vignette gradients. Large blur layers and the extra competing spectre/shine overrides were removed. Artwork overlays are clipped inside the same transformed card shell and require a loaded image.

Decorative effects pause using actual card-shell viewport geometry, IntersectionObserver, document visibility and homepage/search state. Initial visibility is measured immediately; it no longer defaults to paused while waiting for an observer callback. Scroll/resize recovery coalesces into at most one requested frame per burst and never schedules a perpetual loop. Cached image readiness is checked at startup, after API image updates, on pageshow and on visibility restoration. Missing/failed artwork still hides foil and light-map effects. Reduced motion stops decorative animation and tilt. Pointer/touch tilt is event-driven; CSS owns idle movement. Primary touch gestures do not prevent scrolling or pinch zoom and need no device-motion permission. Viewport geometry also supplies offscreen detection when IntersectionObserver is unavailable.

### Artwork-specific lighting fallback

**No window lights or sky mask were fabricated.** The exact source artwork could not be inspected. The Scryfall page returned 403 through web research, image URLs were inaccessible through that tool, and the shell artwork-download command was cancelled at its network permission prompt. A search result from a replica seller was not accepted as an authentic alignment reference. No permission retry or security-setting change was made.

5F.1 adds a pixel-derived green light map **inside the card shell**, using a second SVG image with the identical genuine artwork URL. A static SVG colour matrix extracts `G − 0.5R − 0.5B − 0.015` into alpha, amplifies it by seven, softens edges by 0.65 image units and composites spectral green (#80c990) through that mask. Neutral grey and dark pixels remain transparent. Opacity pulses over 17 seconds, from 0.42 to an occasional 0.78 peak. This enhances existing greenish pixels without a guessed sky polygon or random window lights. The original image stays visible underneath. The SVG uses the same 488×680 coordinate system, inset, image URL and transformed shell, so it follows the source image rather than the viewport. Surrounding background greens/mist/halo are reduced.

This is an image-derived highlight effect, **not a visually verified sky-only mask**. It can also catch green printing details outside the sky; its appearance on the actual source image remains unverified. Individual window locations and asynchronous torchlight are still **INCOMPLETE**. No download/permission prompt was retried in 5F.1. The exact source could not be inspected, so no window coordinates were fabricated. Neither 3D compositing nor the new green/foil intensity has been certified visually.

## Selected-card flipping

`app.js` handles the homepage/search, printing explorer and permanent guides through the existing shared renderer. No guide files were edited. The floating flip control is restricted to a selected printing with a whitelisted `transform`, `modal_dfc`, `reversible_card` or `double_faced_token` layout and exactly two face-image records. Both must be HTTPS Scryfall front/back images whose filenames match that printing UUID. Ambiguous/unsupported image data gets no control. Split, Adventure, Kamigawa `flip`, meld, art-series and ordinary single-faced layouts do not qualify.

The original front is visible while the back loads. Both images must load and decode before the flip button enables. The two surfaces remain mounted, use backface visibility and rotate between 0 and 180 degrees; returning to the front reverses direction. Flipping changes only local surface state, not the selected card, printing UUID, finish, URL or search position. New selections abort obsolete image readiness work; stale controls cannot flip a different card. Missing images, decode failure and timeout preserve the front and report an unavailable flip. Native buttons provide keyboard/touch activation, accessible face labels, pressed state, focus visibility and a 44px touch target around a small icon. Reduced motion removes the rotation transition.

## Minimal finish controls

Large foil buttons and per-row thumbnail toggles are removed. An actively selected foil/etched printing offering nonfoil gets a small red-X control that explicitly selects its real nonfoil finish. Nonfoil-only and foil-only printings have no such control. A compact selected-printing finish dropdown contains only supplied finishes and can restore foil. Printing IDs remain unchanged; exact Deck Studio handoff links and copied URLs reflect the selected finish.

Only the selected printing's thumbnail animates; other table thumbnails have no animated shimmer or floating controls. The main shimmer waits for artwork and pauses offscreen/in hidden tabs. Shared shimmer and featured surface animations use transforms/opacity rather than animated filters. Finishes continue to come from Scryfall, never an inferred treatment. Deck Studio files, quantity/add controls and its internal face navigation are unchanged.

## Floating presentation and translucent haze refinement

The exact LTC #390 artwork, in-card light map and surge-foil gradients/keyframes are preserved. A new weekly-card-stage supplies one 1000px perspective context. Idle movement stays on the outer float surface; pointer/touch tilt stays on the clipped card shell, avoiding competing transforms. Touch tilt is limited to 2° X / 2.5° Y; mobile idle ranges are smaller than desktop. Mobile card widths are capped at 255/228/212px across existing breakpoints, and fixed 520/460/420px column minimum heights are removed for this feature.

The former opaque-looking oval is replaced by three offset, low-alpha green gradients on an unclipped rectangular haze layer. It sits behind the card in the stage, independent of card tilt. A 9.5-second opacity/anisotropic-scale cycle gently expands/contracts it; an 11-second upper-area light variation adds restrained emphasis. No border-radius circle, hard edge, animated blur or artwork replacement is used. A separate black ground shadow varies subtly with the 13-second idle lift. Reduced-motion and offscreen/tab pause rules apply to all new layers. No new animation loop or device-motion permission is required.

Tests check the new depth/haze structure, mobile bounds, reduced touch ranges and preservation of the foil implementation. Visual appearance, clipping and motion quality remain unverified without a real browser; no pixel-perfect claim is made.
