The first of the Markov line

One card. One printing. One corner of Magic worth opening the archive for.

WEEK 004
◇ OPENING THE MARKOV ARCHIVE…

INNISTRAD REMASTERED · SHOWCASE · #328

VAMPIRE KNIGHT · EMINENCE · THE PROGENITOR

Edgar Markov

Edgar Markov does not need to enter the battlefield to begin building his army.

His defining ability, Eminence, works from the command zone as well as the battlefield: every other Vampire spell brings another Vampire with it. By the time Edgar finally attacks, the court he created can already be waiting for him.

First released in Commander 2017, Edgar became one of Magic's defining Vampire commanders. Innistrad Remastered returned him to the plane that made the Markov name famous, including this showcase treatment illustrated by Betty Jiang.

◇
MANASCOUT'S PRINTING

The showcase #328 gets the nod this week. Edgar seated at court with goblets surrounding him feels less like another Vampire card and more like a portrait of the dynasty itself.
