WILDS OF ELDRAINE: ENCHANTING TALES · #71

ENCHANTMENT · A QUESTION EVERY COMMANDER PLAYER KNOWS

Rhystic Study

Three words have followed Rhystic Study from table to table: “Do you pay?” Whenever an opponent casts a spell, the Study offers them a choice — spend one more mana, or let its controller draw a card. Simple text has turned it into one of Magic's most recognisable multiplayer enchantments.

◇

DID YOU KNOW?

The ability triggers when the opponent casts their spell. The choice to pay happens later, when Rhystic Study's triggered ability resolves — and that trigger can still resolve even if the original spell is countered.
