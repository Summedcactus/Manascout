TALES OF MIDDLE-EARTH COMMANDER · #390

MINAS MORGUL · SURGE FOIL · SEAN VO

CABAL COFFERS · REALMS AND RELICS · SURGE FOIL

Minas Morgul

A city once claimed by Gondor, transformed into a place of darkness — and one of Magic's most striking land treatments.

Minas Morgul is the Lord of the Rings-themed version of Cabal Coffers, one of black mana's most famous lands. For two mana and a tap, Cabal Coffers produces one black mana for each Swamp you control.

The card rewards a mana base built around Swamps, making it especially powerful in mono-black Commander decks. What begins as an ordinary land can become a source of enormous mana as the game progresses.

The original Cabal Coffers appeared in Torment in 2002. Its Minas Morgul interpretation arrived in The Lord of the Rings: Tales of Middle-earth Commander. This borderless Realms and Relics treatment was illustrated by Sean Vo.

UNDERSTANDING MAGIC PRINTINGS

One card. Many different versions.

What is an MTG printing?

A printing is a particular release of a Magic: The Gathering card. The same card can appear across multiple sets with different artwork, collector numbers, frames and rarities.

See Rhystic Study printings →

What makes a foil version different?

Foil cards use reflective treatments. Depending on the release, a card may have traditional foil, surge foil, etched foil or another special finish. Not every treatment exists for every printing.

Explore collector treatments →

How do I find the exact version?

Search for the card name, then compare the available printings. Set codes, collector numbers, artwork and treatment details help distinguish one release from another.
