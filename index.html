URZA'S SAGA · #228

SORCERY · SIX MANA · NOTHING LEFT STANDING

Wildfire

Some spells win the game. Others leave almost nothing behind. First appearing in 1998, Wildfire asks every player to sacrifice four lands before dealing four damage to every creature. What remains is a battlefield stripped of resources — and an opportunity for whoever prepared for the devastation.

◇

DID YOU KNOW?

Wildfire became a natural partner for artifact mana and resilient creatures. While opponents lost lands and smaller creatures, a prepared deck could retain enough resources to rebuild first. Its destruction was never entirely indiscriminate.
