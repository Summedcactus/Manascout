SECRET LAIR DROP · DANDÂN · #2143

ISLAND · SPELL RECURSION · UNDERWATER SANCTUARY

Mystic Sanctuary

Sometimes the most dangerous spell in your graveyard is only one land drop away from being drawn again.

Mystic Sanctuary looks like an Island, but in the right deck it behaves like a piece of spell recursion hidden inside the mana base. If it enters untapped while you control three or more other Islands, it can put an instant or sorcery from your graveyard back on top of your library.

First printed in Throne of Eldraine, Mystic Sanctuary later received one of its most distinctive visual treatments through the Secret Lair Dandân release. This borderless foil version, illustrated by Amélie Fléchais, pushes the card fully into its underwater identity.

◇
MANASCOUT'S PRINTING

Secret Lair #2143 gets the pick this week. The submerged architecture, luminous water and dreamlike depth turn Mystic Sanctuary from a useful blue land into something that genuinely feels like a hidden place beneath the sea.
