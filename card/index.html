WHY PRINTINGS MATTER

The same card can be a completely different choice

Magic cards can accumulate years or even decades of reprints. A single card name may cover old-border versions, modern frames, alternative illustrations, foil finishes, borderless treatments, promotional releases and collector-focused editions.

That means searching only for the card name can hide the part that actually matters when you are buying or collecting: which printing is the one you want?

ManaScout is built around that decision. The permanent card guides add context around notable releases, while the live explorer lets you compare available printings directly and continue scouting through related cards.
