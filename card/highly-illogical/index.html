Highly Illogical

The standard Highly Illogical is TRK #62, an uncommon illustrated by Joel Chaim Holtzman. It debuted with Star Trek on 13 November 2026. The extended-art Commander Party promo is PW26 #17, illustrated by Patricia Pria. Both exact records offer Nonfoil and Traditional Foil.

Identifying your copy

Check TRK #62 or PW26 #17 first

The foil surface is insufficient for identification because both records come in foil. Check the set code, number and artist together. Patricia Pria’s artwork and the extended frame identify the PW26 #17 promo. A foil with Holtzman’s art and TRK #62 is the standard set foil.

How Wizards distributed the event promo

Wizards announced extended-art promos through the Wizards Play Network, with Nonfoil copies at most participating retailers and Traditional Foil copies at WPN Premium locations. The announced Commander Party windows were 20–26 November 2026 and 15–21 January 2027, and participants could receive either Highly Illogical or Munitions Enthusiast. That distribution applies to the event promo. It does not describe the ordinary TRK #62 booster printing or guarantee which promo a location has.

Check the same rules across frames

Highly Illogical can counter a spell with mana value two or less. Kicking it removes that restriction. For another Star Trek case where the words on the card matter to identification, see the Hit It! Oracle update.
