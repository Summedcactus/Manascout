PRINTING HISTORY

Jace, the Mind Sculptor across its releases

◇

Jace, the Mind Sculptor first appeared in Worldwake in 2010. With four loyalty abilities and a major impact on competitive Magic, it quickly became one of the game's most recognisable planeswalkers.

Since then, Jace has returned in a number of releases, giving the card several different visual identities through new artwork, frames and premium treatments.

That combination of competitive history and collector appeal makes Jace especially interesting to compare by printing rather than just by card name.

VERSIONS & TREATMENTS

What changes between Jace, the Mind Sculptor printings?

◇

Different Jace printings can vary through artwork, frame treatment, set identity, finish and collector presentation while keeping the same underlying card.

Some versions preserve the look of the original Worldwake release, while others take a more modern or premium approach with alternative artwork and different frames.

For a planeswalker with such a recognisable character, artwork and presentation can be a major part of deciding which version belongs in a deck or collection.

WHICH ONE?

Which Jace, the Mind Sculptor printing should you choose?

◇

For a deck copy: compare the lower-priced available printings first if your priority is simply playing Jace rather than chasing a premium version.

For original character: the Worldwake-era presentation may appeal most if you want the version associated with Jace's original release.

For artwork: compare the different illustrations directly, because Jace's alternative artwork can change the feel of the card considerably.

For collecting: premium finishes and special treatments can make certain printings stand apart much more clearly than standard copies.
