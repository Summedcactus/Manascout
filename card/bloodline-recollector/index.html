A prepare card with ordinary and serialized printings

Bloodline Recollector debuted in Reality Fracture on 2 October 2026 as FRA #49, a mythic rare illustrated by Cynthia Sheppard. The full catalogue name is Bloodline Recollector // Ancestral Craving: its prepare spell is part of this gameplay identity, not an unrelated reprint.

Wizards specifies 500 serialized versions of the #402 headliner, in double rainbow foil with Mark Poole’s artwork and a throwback full-art frame. Those English-only cards appear exclusively in Reality Fracture Collector Boosters of any language. Ordinary #49, extended-art #427 and promo #49p are not subject to that 500-copy count.

Sheppard’s original and Poole’s headliner

The regular #49 and extended-art #427 credit Cynthia Sheppard. The extended frame is a different presentation of that artwork, while promo-pack PFRA #49p adds its own stamped identity. PFRA and FRA are different set codes; the suffix matters.

Mark Poole’s #402 is a distinct illustration and serialized treatment. The throwback appearance references a collecting style; it does not make this Ancestral Recall or create a second gameplay card. The catalogue UUID identifies a printing family, not a specific individual serial number.

500 serialized copies, not 500 cards in total

Normal and extended-art records offer nonfoil and traditional foil; the checked promo-pack record also offers both. The headliner #402 is double rainbow foil only. Do not label a traditional foil #49 as serialized or mistake extended art for the headliner’s throwback full-art frame.

Compare #49 and #427 in nonfoil first to judge the frame. Then compare the Poole headliner as a separate art-and-surface choice. No serial-specific authenticity, available stock or valuation claim is made here.

Bloodline Recollector and Ancestral Craving belong together

The {1}{B} Vampire Warlock becomes prepared at an end step if three or more creatures died that turn. While prepared, its reminder text permits casting a copy of its spell; doing so unprepares it. Ancestral Craving costs {B}, draws a target player three cards and makes that player lose 3 life.

This is a prepare layout, not a conventional transforming two-sided card. The shared explorer uses the full catalogue identity so exact edition, language and finish stay linked.
