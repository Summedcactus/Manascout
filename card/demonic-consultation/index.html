PRINTING HISTORY

Demonic Consultation across its releases

◇

Demonic Consultation first appeared in Ice Age in 1995. Its unusual mix of extreme efficiency and extreme risk made it one of black's most memorable tutor-style spells.

Compared with cards that have been reprinted constantly over decades, Demonic Consultation has a much smaller printing history.

That makes the differences between individual releases more noticeable, especially for players choosing between older printings and newer collector-focused versions.

VERSIONS & TREATMENTS

What changes between Demonic Consultation printings?

◇

Different Demonic Consultation printings can vary in artwork, frame style, set identity, finish and overall presentation while keeping the same underlying effect.

Earlier versions offer the visual identity of older Magic, while later releases can provide cleaner modern frames or more distinctive collector treatments.

Because there are fewer printings than cards such as Counterspell or Sol Ring, each release represents a larger part of Demonic Consultation's overall printing history.
