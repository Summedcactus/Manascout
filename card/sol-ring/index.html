MANASCOUT GUIDE

One of Magic's most famous mana rocks

◇

Sol Ring is a one-mana artifact that taps for two colourless mana, allowing its controller to accelerate their mana from the moment it enters the battlefield.

That simple exchange — spending one mana for an artifact capable of producing two — has made Sol Ring one of the most recognisable mana rocks in Magic: The Gathering.

Sol Ring is especially associated with Commander, where its explosive early-game acceleration and extensive history of reprints have made it one of the format's best-known cards.

PRINTING HISTORY

Sol Ring's original MTG printing

◇

Sol Ring first appeared in Magic's Limited Edition Alpha in 1993. It therefore reaches back to the very beginning of Magic: The Gathering and has remained one of the game's most recognisable artifacts.

Unlike cards with only a handful of releases, Sol Ring has accumulated a large printing history. Later versions have appeared with different artwork, frames, set symbols and finishes, creating plenty of choices for players and collectors.

REPRINTS & VERSIONS

Why are there so many Sol Ring printings?

◇

Sol Ring has been reprinted many times across Magic's history, with Commander products contributing heavily to the number of versions available. Its repeated appearances have produced a broad selection of traditional and collectible printings.

Depending on the release, a Sol Ring can differ in artwork, frame treatment, finish and presentation. That makes comparing Sol Ring printings useful even when every version represents the same underlying Magic card.
