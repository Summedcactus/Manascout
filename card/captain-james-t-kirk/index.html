First printing and the signed headliner

Captain James T. Kirk debuted in Star Trek on 13 November 2026. TRK #142 is the regular mythic rare, illustrated by Jason Pastrana. The four English physical records checked here share one Oracle identity and rules text; #1701 is a treatment of this card, not a new Kirk with different abilities.

The signed headliner pairs Chris Rallis’s portrait with William Shatner’s autograph. Rallis is the illustrator; Shatner is the signer. Wizards describes approximately 250 copies of each of seven signed headliners, in English, randomly inserted in Collector Boosters of any language. That figure does not limit ordinary Kirk printings.

Pastrana, Newton and Rallis are three artwork choices

Regular #142 establishes the original image. Showcase #418 changes to Josh Newton’s Classic Enterprise artwork. #542 uses that same painting with surge foil: it is a separate collector record and surface, rather than another illustration.

The headliner #1701 is the signed portrait edition. A regular foil Kirk is not a signed headliner. An artist signature acquired separately on another printing does not change that card into the factory-distributed headliner record.

Traditional foil, surge foil and signed headliner

Choose nonfoil or traditional foil for #142 and #418. #542 is surge foil only; #1701 is a foil-only signed headliner. The catalogue’s broad foil flag alone does not distinguish those surfaces or prove an autograph.

For a deck copy, compare #142 nonfoil with #418 nonfoil first. Holding finish constant reveals the actual art and frame preference; inspect #542 only if surge is the finish you want. The signed edition is a separate collecting decision, not necessary to use Kirk’s rules.

Identify the gameplay card before choosing the surface

This mono-red legendary Human Officer costs {2}{R}. Its enters-or-attacks trigger offers looting, an Officer token or a temporary team power bonus; an empty hand permits one or more modes. The card’s shortened self-reference to Captain Kirk is not a different collector identity.

Other Star Trek characters have their own rules and printing families. Do not substitute a Picard or Spock record merely because it has a similar showcase name. Current legality belongs to the selected live record; the guide does not certify formats.
