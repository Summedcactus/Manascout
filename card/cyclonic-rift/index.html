PRINTING HISTORY

Cyclonic Rift across its different releases

◇

Cyclonic Rift has become one of Magic's most recognisable blue Commander cards. Over time, additional releases have given players and collectors more versions to choose from rather than a single presentation of the card.

Those versions can differ in artwork, frame treatment, set appearance, finish and overall collectability. That means choosing a Cyclonic Rift is not necessarily just about finding the card itself — it can also mean deciding which printing best fits your deck or collection.

VERSIONS & TREATMENTS

What changes between Cyclonic Rift printings?

Different Cyclonic Rift printings can offer different artwork, frames, finishes and presentation styles while still representing the same underlying Magic card.
