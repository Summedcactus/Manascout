MTG PRINTING GUIDE / STAR TREK

Hit It!

Hit It! debuted on 13 November 2026 as the rare TRK #240. The extended-art TRK #476 is from the same release. Both use Aaron J. Riley’s illustration of Captain Pike and come in Nonfoil and Traditional Foil. The number and frame identify the edition.

Identifying your copy

Printed text and current Oracle wording

The scans of both printings say to look at the top five cards. The updated Oracle instruction is to reveal those five cards. If any is a land, you must put one onto the battlefield tapped. Put the remaining cards into your graveyard. Wizards also confirms this is not a mill effect. The wording update applies equally to #240 and #476.

Identify the frame before choosing foil

Look for the art running farther into the side frame on #476. Both collector numbers can be Nonfoil or Traditional Foil. A foil surface alone does not tell you whether you have #240 or #476.
