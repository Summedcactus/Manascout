MTG PRINTING GUIDE

Mystic Sanctuary

Mystic Sanctuary has a compact printing history, with most ordinary reprints preserving Randy Vargas’ original landscape. The meaningful collector alternatives are its Time Spiral Remastered retro frame and Amélie Fléchais’s foil-only Dandân illustration. A few precise comparisons are more useful here than inventing a long chain of premium editions.

When was Mystic Sanctuary first printed?

◇

Mystic Sanctuary first appeared in Throne of Eldraine (ELD), 4 October 2019. The original record is ELD #247, at common, illustrated by Randy Vargas. Original finish availability: nonfoil, traditional foil.

HOW IT WORKS

Three other Islands and an untapped arrival

◇

Mystic Sanctuary has no mana cost and is a nonbasic Land — Island with a blue colour identity. Its Island subtype supplies the ability to tap for blue mana; it is not a basic land.

It enters tapped unless you control three or more other Islands. If it enters untapped, its triggered ability may put a target instant or sorcery from your graveyard on top of your library. It returns the card to the library, not to your hand. Entering tapped does not produce that trigger.

PRINTING HISTORY

One landscape, a retro frame and a later new illustration

◇

Throne of Eldraine (ELD) #247 debuted on 4 October 2019 at common with Randy Vargas art. Both nonfoil and traditional foil exist.

Time Spiral Remastered #408 followed on 19 March 2021. It keeps the original illustration but uses a retro frame and is catalogued at special rarity, with nonfoil and traditional foil. That later retro frame is not evidence of a pre-2019 original printing.

Murders at Karlov Manor Commander #277 and Mystery Booster 2 #110 are 2024 nonfoil reprints of the original artwork. These provide release identity choices without establishing additional paintings.

Secret Lair’s Dandân Deck adds SLD #2143, catalogued on 1 December 2025. Amélie Fléchais supplies its borderless illustration, and the record is foil-only. The complete catalogue also lists a later Secrets of Strixhaven Commander reprint; the table concentrates on the original and the clearest physical alternatives.

ARTWORK & FRAMES

Frame nostalgia versus a different painting

◇

ELD #247, TSR #408, MKC #277 and MB2 #110 credit Vargas. The retro TSR frame changes presentation rather than the illustration. Compare those records for frame and product identity.

Fléchais’s SLD #2143 is the actual artwork departure in this shortlist. Its stylised Dandân presentation and foil-only surface are separate properties, even though both arrive on the same record.

FOILS & PREMIUM FINISHES

Two ordinary foil routes and the Dandân foil

◇

ELD #247 and TSR #408 offer nonfoil and traditional foil. MKC #277 and MB2 #110 are nonfoil-only. SLD #2143 is traditional-foil-only; no nonfoil counterpart for that precise number was established.

A retro-frame foil, a borderless foil and a regular-frame foil describe different choices here. This review establishes no etched, surge, confetti or serialized Mystic Sanctuary.

WHICH PRINTING?

Which Mystic Sanctuary printing should you choose?

◇

For the original: ELD #247 preserves the debut release, with a choice of nonfoil or foil.

For a retro-frame deck: TSR #408 is the relevant later edition. Choose it for its frame rather than treating it as an older first printing.

For alternate art: SLD #2143 is the Fléchais Dandân record, and requires a foil preference. For a straightforward nonfoil reprint, compare MKC #277 or MB2 #110.

◇ MANASCOUT PERSPECTIVE

ManaScout pick: Dandân #2143 for the illustration

◇

Our artwork-led pick remains SLD #2143. Fléchais’s image and borderless presentation give the card a different identity from the Vargas reprints. If you want nonfoil, compare ELD #247 and TSR #408 instead.

Legality checked 4 November 2026: Mystic Sanctuary appears on the official Modern and Pauper banned lists. Its original common rarity does not make it currently Pauper-legal. The catalogue lists it as Commander-legal; each format must be checked independently.
