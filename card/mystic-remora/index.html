PRINTING HISTORY

Mystic Remora across its releases

◇

Mystic Remora first appeared in Ice Age in 1995. Its low mana cost and ability to draw cards when opponents cast noncreature spells helped it become a familiar source of card advantage in multiplayer Magic.

For a long time, Mystic Remora had a relatively limited printing history compared with many other popular blue cards.

Later releases expanded the range of artwork, frames and finishes available, giving players more choice between classic presentation and newer treatments.
