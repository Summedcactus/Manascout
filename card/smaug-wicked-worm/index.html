HOW IT WORKS

Tapped Treasure now, a resource engine for later spells

◇

Smaug, Wicked Worm costs {3}{B}{R} and is a 5/5 Legendary Creature — Dragon with flying and a black-red colour identity. Its enters ability creates tapped Treasures equal to the artifacts your opponents control.

Its other ability triggers when you cast a spell using mana from a Treasure: you draw a card and lose one life. That condition checks Treasure mana used to cast the spell, not simply whether you control Treasures. The initial tokens entering tapped distinguishes this card from Dockside Extortionist.

PRINTING HISTORY

Three collector records in one Hobbit release family

◇

The original is The Hobbit (HOB) #164, released on 14 August 2026 at rare, illustrated by Antonio José Manzanedo. Nonfoil and traditional foil exist.

HOB #245 is the borderless book-cover version illustrated by Tyler Jacobson, also available in nonfoil and traditional foil. #281 is its surge-foil counterpart and shares Jacobson’s illustration. These are same-release treatments, not a sequence of later-set reprints.

The product is The Hobbit, with set code HOB. It should not be relabelled as an LTC printing from the 2023 Tales of Middle-earth Commander catalogue merely because both products depict Tolkien’s world.

ARTWORK & FRAMES

Two paintings, not three different Smaug identities

◇

Manzanedo’s #164 is the regular-frame debut. Jacobson’s #245 uses the book-cover presentation; #281 adds surge foil to that same artwork family. The catalogue’s poster-style tag and Wizards’ book-cover description refer to the presentation, rather than another card name.

Other cards named Smaug are different designs. In particular, Wizards’ gleaming-gold Smaug the Magnificent headliner is not a premium printing of Smaug, Wicked Worm. Do not transfer its artist, collector number, finish or approximate print run onto this guide.

FOILS & PREMIUM FINISHES

Traditional foil and surge foil have separate records

◇

#164 and #245 both offer nonfoil and traditional foil. #281 is surge foil only. A traditional-foil #245 is not the same finish as #281, although the Jacobson artwork is shared.

Wizards places surge-foil book-cover cards in Collector Boosters. This does not establish a gleaming-gold, etched, fracture or serialized Wicked Worm. A treatment offered for another Smaug card cannot be inferred for this one.

WHICH PRINTING?

Which Smaug, Wicked Worm printing should you choose?

◇

For the original: #164 preserves Manzanedo’s regular presentation. Choose the finish separately.

For book-cover artwork: #245 is the Jacobson record, with nonfoil and traditional foil choices.

For surge specifically: identify #281, then inspect the actual card’s surface and condition. Stock renders cannot show its physical reflectivity.
