Smaug, Wicked Worm in The Hobbit

◇

Smaug, Wicked Worm debuted in The Hobbit in 2026. Unlike long-running Magic staples with decades of reprints, Smaug currently has a compact printing history concentrated within a single release.

The available versions include a regular treatment and alternative borderless presentations, giving collectors meaningful visual choices despite the card being relatively new.

That smaller number of printings makes Smaug particularly easy to compare side by side: the differences are focused on artwork, framing and finish rather than years of reprint history.

VERSIONS & TREATMENTS

What changes between Smaug, Wicked Worm printings?

◇

Smaug's printings differ mainly through artwork, border treatment and finish rather than through different sets or long-separated release eras.

The standard version uses one illustration, while the borderless releases offer a different presentation and artwork. A premium foil-only treatment also gives collectors a more distinctive version to consider.

Because every version comes from the same release, choosing between them is largely about which depiction of Smaug and which treatment you prefer.

WHICH ONE?

Which Smaug, Wicked Worm printing should you choose?

◇

For a deck copy: start with the regular printing if your main priority is simply playing Smaug without paying for a premium treatment.

For artwork: compare the regular and borderless versions directly, as the different illustrations give the card a noticeably different visual identity.

For presentation: the borderless treatment gives Smaug more room visually and may suit a showcase Commander deck better than the standard frame.

For collecting: the premium foil treatment is the version to examine if you want the most visually distinctive release rather than the straightforward play copy.
