When was Arcane Signet first printed?

◇

Arcane Signet was first released in 2019 with the Throne of Eldraine Brawl decks. It was one of the cards created specifically for those multiplayer-focused preconstructed decks rather than appearing in regular Throne of Eldraine Draft Boosters.

Its two-mana cost and ability to produce a mana of any colour in your commander's colour identity made it an immediate fit for Commander, where it went on to receive a long run of reprints and special treatments.

MANASCOUT GUIDE

From Brawl-deck newcomer to Commander essential

◇

Arcane Signet is a two-mana artifact that taps for one mana of any colour in your commander's colour identity.

It debuted in the 2019 Throne of Eldraine Brawl decks, but its design translated almost perfectly into Commander: inexpensive acceleration, flexible fixing and no colour commitment in the casting cost.

That combination helped Arcane Signet move from a Brawl product card into one of the most recognisable mana rocks associated with Commander.

Its popularity also means there is now a much wider range of Arcane Signet printings to compare, from straightforward preconstructed-deck copies to releases built around distinctive artwork and premium presentation.

COMMANDER RULES

How does Arcane Signet work with colour identity?

◇

Arcane Signet taps to add one mana of any colour in your commander's colour identity . In a deck with two commanders, it can use the colours in their combined colour identities.

A colourless commander does not make Arcane Signet produce colourless mana. If your commander's colour identity contains no colours, Arcane Signet produces no mana.

The same is true if you are playing a game without a commander: Arcane Signet's mana ability has no colour available to produce.

FIRST PRINTING

The original Arcane Signet printing

◇

The original Arcane Signet is the 2019 Throne of Eldraine Brawl-deck printing.

If you want Arcane Signet's first printing rather than one of the many later Commander reprints, that Brawl release is the version to look for.

The first printing is also a snapshot of Arcane Signet before it became a near-default inclusion across huge numbers of Commander products and decks.

ARCANE SIGNET REPRINTS

What changes between Arcane Signet reprints?

◇

Arcane Signet's rules text is simple, but its many reprints can look dramatically different from one another.

The main differences between versions can include artwork, frame style, set symbol, foil availability, borderless treatment and crossover presentation .

That gives players a choice between inexpensive deck copies, versions tied to particular Commander products and more collector-focused releases.

ART & TREATMENTS

Arcane Signet Secret Lair, borderless art and foils

◇

Arcane Signet has moved well beyond its original preconstructed deck appearance and now has versions aimed squarely at players who care about how a staple looks on the battlefield.

The 2020 Secret Lair: A Box of Rocks drop included an alternative-art Arcane Signet in both foil and non-foil editions alongside other famous mana rocks.

Commander Masters later gave Arcane Signet a borderless treatment, including a traditional-foil borderless version, creating another visually distinct option for Commander decks.

In 2026, Secret Lair x My Little Pony: Friendship is Magic added another crossover Arcane Signet treatment, again offered as part of foil and non-foil drops.
