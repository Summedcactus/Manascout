PRINTING HISTORY

Deflecting Swat and its Commander-era releases

◇

Deflecting Swat first appeared in Commander 2020. Its alternate-cost ability quickly made it one of the most recognisable red interaction spells in Commander.

Compared with long-running cards such as Counterspell or Birds of Paradise, Deflecting Swat has a much shorter printing history.

That smaller pool of versions makes the differences between individual releases easier to compare, especially when artwork, finish and presentation are the main reasons to prefer one copy over another.

VERSIONS & TREATMENTS

What changes between Deflecting Swat printings?

◇

Deflecting Swat versions can differ in artwork, set presentation, frame treatment and finish while keeping the same underlying rules text.

With fewer releases than many older staples, the choice is less about navigating decades of reprints and more about deciding whether you want a straightforward deck copy or a more distinctive treatment.
