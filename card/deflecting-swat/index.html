When was Deflecting Swat first printed?

◇

Deflecting Swat was first printed in Commander 2020 in 2020. The original printing is collector number 50 and features artwork by Izzy.

The Commander 2020 version was originally available only as a non-foil card, before later releases introduced foil, foil-etched, borderless and alternative-art treatments.

MANASCOUT GUIDE

One of Commander's defining free interaction spells

◇

Deflecting Swat is a three-mana red instant that allows you to choose new targets for a target spell or ability.

Its defining feature is its alternative cost: if you control a commander, you may cast Deflecting Swat without paying its mana cost.

That gives red Commander decks access to powerful interaction without needing to leave mana open, allowing an opponent's removal spell, targeted ability or other effect to be redirected at a legal new target.

The combination of zero-mana interaction and flexible redirection quickly made Deflecting Swat one of the most recognisable cards introduced through Commander 2020.

HOW IT WORKS

How does Deflecting Swat work?

◇

If you control a commander while casting Deflecting Swat, you may cast the spell without paying its mana cost.

You then choose a target spell or ability and may change its targets, provided the new targets are legal.

Deflecting Swat does not change how many targets the original spell or ability has. It only lets you replace existing targets with new legal ones.

You also only need to control a commander. The rules do not require that commander to be the one you started the game with.

COMMANDER STAPLE

Why is Deflecting Swat so powerful in Commander?

◇

Commander makes Deflecting Swat's alternative cost relatively easy to enable because the format is already built around keeping a commander available.

Once the commander is on the battlefield, Deflecting Swat can function as zero-mana interaction.

That can protect a commander or another important permanent, interfere with an opponent's combo, or turn a powerful targeted spell or ability back against another legal target.

The possibility of Deflecting Swat also changes how opponents have to evaluate interaction even when its controller appears to be completely tapped out.

FIRST PRINTING

The original Commander 2020 Deflecting Swat

◇

Deflecting Swat first appeared in Commander 2020.

The original printing is collector number 50, is illustrated by Izzy and was released as a non-foil card.

It was included in the Arcane Maelstrom Commander deck alongside Kalamax, the Stormsire.

If you specifically want the first Deflecting Swat rather than a later premium treatment, Commander 2020 #50 is therefore the printing to look for.

COMMANDER MASTERS

Deflecting Swat returns in Commander Masters

◇

Deflecting Swat received its first major reprint in Commander Masters in 2023.

The standard Commander Masters printing is collector number 214 and retains the original Izzy artwork.

Unlike the Commander 2020 original, the Commander Masters release expanded Deflecting Swat into multiple collector treatments rather than offering only one conventional version.

That made Commander Masters the release where Deflecting Swat's printing history moved from a simple reprint into genuine version comparison.

FOIL-ETCHED

Commander Masters foil-etched Deflecting Swat

◇

Commander Masters collector number 532 is the foil-etched Deflecting Swat.

It keeps the Izzy artwork associated with the original printing but presents the card through a different frame treatment and foil-etched finish.

That makes it particularly useful for players who like the original art but want a more premium version than the Commander 2020 card.

BORDERLESS

Commander Masters borderless Deflecting Swat

◇

Commander Masters also introduced a completely different borderless Deflecting Swat.

Collector number 698 features artwork by Greg Staples, giving the card a new visual identity rather than simply applying a premium finish to the original illustration.

The artwork pushes beyond the conventional frame and is available in both foil and non-foil versions.

For players searching specifically for a Deflecting Swat borderless, Commander Masters #698 is the key printing to compare.

SECRET LAIR

Deflecting Swat Secret Lair printing

◇

Deflecting Swat received another major alternative-art treatment through Secret Lair in 2025.

The Secret Lair printing is collector number 1552 and features artwork by Jesper Ejsing.

It appeared as part of Artist Series: Jesper Ejsing and was offered in both non-foil and rainbow-foil versions.

Because it uses completely different artwork from both Izzy's original and Greg Staples' Commander Masters version, the Secret Lair card gives collectors another genuinely distinct Deflecting Swat rather than just another finish.

AVATAR

Deflecting Swat Avatar: The Last Airbender version

◇

Deflecting Swat later received a Magic: The Gathering | Avatar: The Last Airbender treatment.

The Avatar: The Last Airbender Eternal printing is collector number 311 and features artwork by Patrick Gañas.

Its flavour text references Iroh and the redirection of lightning, making the crossover an especially natural thematic match for Deflecting Swat's ability to redirect targeted effects.

The Avatar version provides another choice for players who want Deflecting Swat to look completely different from its original Commander-era presentation.

DEFLECTING SWAT REPRINTS

What changes between Deflecting Swat printings?

◇

Deflecting Swat has a much shorter printing history than cards such as Sol Ring or Lightning Bolt, but its small catalogue already covers several substantially different treatments.

Differences can include artwork, frame design, foil finish, foil-etched treatment, borderless presentation and crossover styling .

The Commander 2020 original gives you the first printing, Commander Masters offers both upgraded finishes and new art, Secret Lair adds an artist-focused interpretation, and Avatar gives the card a crossover treatment.

That makes Deflecting Swat particularly easy to compare: there are not hundreds of versions to navigate, but the versions that do exist are meaningfully different.

ART & TREATMENTS

Deflecting Swat artwork, foils and premium versions

◇

Deflecting Swat's versions now cover several different interpretations of the same core idea: turning an opponent's targeted effect somewhere else.

Izzy defines the original Commander 2020 card and regular Commander Masters reprint.

Greg Staples provides the Commander Masters borderless interpretation, while Jesper Ejsing and Patrick Gañas give the card two further alternative-art identities.

Combined with standard foil, foil-etched and rainbow-foil finishes, Deflecting Swat now has enough variety that choosing the card and choosing the printing are two separate decisions.

WHICH ONE?

Which Deflecting Swat printing should you choose?

◇

For the original: choose Commander 2020 #50 from 2020.

For the original artwork with a newer printing: compare Commander Masters #214.

For foil-etched: look at Commander Masters #532.

For borderless artwork: compare the Greg Staples Commander Masters #698 treatment.

For Secret Lair: the Jesper Ejsing #1552 printing offers completely different artwork in non-foil and rainbow foil.

For crossover art: the Avatar: The Last Airbender Eternal #311 version ties Deflecting Swat's redirection effect directly into Iroh's lightning-redirection theme.
