PRINTING HISTORY

Jeska's Will across its releases

◇

Jeska's Will first appeared in Commander Legends in 2020. Its ability to generate red mana, exile cards for temporary access and potentially do both with a commander in play quickly made it one of red's most recognisable Commander spells.

Compared with older staples, Jeska's Will has a much shorter printing history, but later releases have still created meaningful differences in artwork, finish and presentation.
