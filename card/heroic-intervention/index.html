Heroic Intervention

Heroic Intervention first appeared in Aether Revolt on 20 January 2017. AER #109 is a rare illustrated by James Ryman, available in Nonfoil and Traditional Foil. The same illustration appears on the Etched Foil CMM #563, so use the set code and number to tell them apart.

Identifying your copy

Which James Ryman version is this?

Check the bottom line before judging the surface. AER #109, M21 #375 and the two Commander Masters records shown here reuse the same illustration. M21 #375 extends the art into the side borders. Regular CMM #295 comes in Nonfoil or Traditional Foil. Use CMM #563 when looking for the matte-frame etched treatment described by Wizards.

Separate crossover art from foil treatment

Josh Hass’s Fallout illustration appears on both PIP #471 and #999. The latter is the extended-art Surge Foil record. For Russell Dauterman’s Countdown art, SLC #66 offers Nonfoil and Traditional Foil, while #97 is the separate Surge Foil record. Marvel’s Source Material variants #78–#80 feature different comic art. The Secret Lair card that reads Aerith’s Curaga Magic is SLD #1872 and shares the Heroic Intervention gameplay identity.

Quick gameplay check

Heroic Intervention gives your permanents hexproof and indestructible until end of turn. Effects that exile without targeting can still remove them. The catalogue lists Heroic Intervention as Commander-legal on the review date. For a different protection effect, compare Teferi’s Protection. Deflecting Swat instead changes a spell or ability’s targets.
