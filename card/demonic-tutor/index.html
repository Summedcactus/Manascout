PRINTING HISTORY

When was Demonic Tutor first printed?

◇

Demonic Tutor first appeared in Limited Edition Alpha in 1993.

Its ability to search your library for any card helped make it one of Magic's most famous black spells.

Later releases have repeatedly revisited the card with new artwork and presentation, creating a printing history that looks very different from the original Alpha version.
