Identifying your copy

Read the number, then check the frame

TRK #263 and #479 show the station from the same viewpoint. The artwork extends farther on #479, and both records offer Nonfoil and Traditional Foil. The number is the reliable way to tell which one you have when the card is sleeved.

Identify the Bundle schematic

Look for a technical schematic and Scott Okumura’s credit on #556. Wizards lists this treatment in the Beam Me Up Bundle’s Traditional Foil promo pool. That is a separate record from the two Paolo Puggioni printings. A foil #263 or #479 does not become a Bundle promo.

Compare other Star Trek collector treatments

Mr. Spock, Science Officer is an example where a Collector Booster Surge Foil has its own number. Highly Illogical has a separately numbered store-event promo. The product channel makes these distinctions useful when looking up a copy.
