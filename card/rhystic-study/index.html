Rhystic Study is a blue enchantment first released in Prophecy in 2000. Its simple tax-or-draw ability has since made it one of the most recognisable card-draw engines in multiplayer Magic.

Whenever an opponent casts a spell, Rhystic Study asks whether that player will pay an additional mana. If they do not, its controller may draw a card — creating the question Commander players have heard countless times: “Do you pay the one?”

Over the years Rhystic Study has received numerous reprints and alternative treatments, giving collectors and players a wide range of artwork, frames and finishes to choose from. ManaScout brings those versions together below so they can be explored and compared in one place.
