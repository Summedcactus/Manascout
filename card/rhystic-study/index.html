MANASCOUT GUIDE

From Prophecy rare to Commander icon

◇

Rhystic Study is a blue enchantment first released in Prophecy in 2000. Its tax-or-draw ability has since made it one of the most recognisable card-advantage engines in multiplayer Magic.

Whenever an opponent casts a spell, that player may pay an additional mana. If they do not, Rhystic Study's controller may draw a card.

That simple trigger produced one of Commander's most familiar table questions: “Do you pay the one?”

Its growth from an older-set enchantment into a major Commander staple has also created a much broader range of Rhystic Study printings to compare.

FIRST PRINTING

The original Rhystic Study printing

◇

The original Rhystic Study is the Prophecy printing from 2000.

If you specifically want Rhystic Study's first printing, Prophecy is therefore the release to look for rather than one of its later reprints.

That version also predates Rhystic Study's modern reputation as a Commander staple, which gives the original printing a different character from later versions created after the card became widely recognised.

What changes between Rhystic Study reprints?

◇

A Rhystic Study reprint may keep the same game text while looking significantly different from the Prophecy original.
