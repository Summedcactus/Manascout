PRINTING HISTORY

Birds of Paradise across Magic's history

◇

Birds of Paradise reaches all the way back to Magic's original Limited Edition Alpha release in 1993. That makes it one of the game's longest-running and most recognisable mana creatures.

Its long history has produced a wide range of reprints across different eras of Magic, with changes in artwork, frames, set symbols and finishes along the way.

Few cards give players quite as much choice between classic presentation and newer collectible treatments, which makes Birds of Paradise particularly interesting to compare by printing rather than just by card name.

VERSIONS & TREATMENTS

What changes between Birds of Paradise printings?

◇

Birds of Paradise has appeared through several distinct visual eras of Magic, so different versions can feel dramatically different even though the underlying card remains the same.

Artwork is one of the biggest differences, but frame style, set identity, foil availability and other premium treatments can also affect how a particular printing looks and feels.

That gives players the option to choose a simple deck copy, a nostalgic older printing or something more visually striking for a favourite deck or collection.

WHICH ONE?

Which Birds of Paradise printing should you choose?

◇

For a deck copy: compare the lower-priced available versions first. Birds of Paradise has enough reprints that you do not necessarily need to pay collector prices to play one.

For artwork: Birds of Paradise is especially worth browsing visually. Its different illustrations give the same card very different personalities across Magic's history.

For nostalgia: older-frame and earlier-era versions can appeal to players who prefer the look and feel of classic Magic.

For collecting: foil versions and more unusual treatments can offer a more distinctive option than a standard reprint.
