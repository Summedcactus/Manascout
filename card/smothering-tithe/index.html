PRINTING HISTORY

Smothering Tithe across its releases

◇

Smothering Tithe first appeared in Ravnica Allegiance in 2019. Its ability to create Treasure whenever an opponent draws a card unless they pay extra mana quickly made it one of white's most recognisable multiplayer resource engines.

Since its original release, Smothering Tithe has appeared in additional products with new artwork, frames and premium treatments.

That gives players several distinct ways to represent the same Commander staple, from straightforward deck copies to more elaborate collector-focused versions.
