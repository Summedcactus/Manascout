PRINTING HISTORY

Force of Will across its releases

◇

Force of Will first appeared in Alliances in 1996. Its ability to counter a spell without paying its normal mana cost helped make it one of the most recognisable blue interaction spells in Magic.

Over time, Force of Will has returned in a relatively selective range of releases rather than being reprinted constantly.

That has produced a printing history with several visually distinct versions, including different artwork, frames and premium treatments.

VERSIONS & TREATMENTS

What changes between Force of Will printings?

◇

Force of Will printings can differ through artwork, frame style, set identity, finish and collector treatment while preserving the same underlying card.

The original Alliances version carries the character of mid-1990s Magic, while later releases offer very different visual interpretations and presentation styles.

That makes Force of Will particularly interesting for players who care about the appearance of a deck as much as the function of the card itself.

WHICH ONE?

Which Force of Will printing should you choose?

◇

For a deck copy: compare the lower-priced available releases first if your priority is simply getting Force of Will into a deck.

For classic Magic: the original-era presentation may appeal most if you prefer older artwork, borders and the visual identity of early Magic.

For artwork: Force of Will has several striking visual interpretations, making artwork one of the strongest reasons to prefer one printing over another.

For collecting: premium finishes and alternative treatments can make certain versions considerably more distinctive than standard copies.
