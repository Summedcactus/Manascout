PRINTING HISTORY

Dockside Extortionist across its releases

◇

Dockside Extortionist first appeared in Commander 2019. Its ability to generate Treasure based on opposing artifacts and enchantments quickly made it one of the most recognisable red cards in Commander.

Compared with older staples, Dockside Extortionist has a much shorter printing history, but its popularity has still produced multiple versions and collector-focused treatments.
