Dominaria United first printing and later Stardates

Sheoldred, the Apocalypse debuted in Dominaria United on 9 September 2022. DMU #107 is the mythic rare original, illustrated by Chris Rahn, in nonfoil and traditional foil. The original, promo, showcase and later crossover records remain distinct collector identities.

SDS #11 arrived with Star Trek Stardates on 13 November 2026, illustrated by Nicky Barkla. It is a reprint of Sheoldred’s gameplay identity, not a newly named Star Trek creature. SDS is the collector set code, not TRK; shared release timing does not make it a main-set TRK printing.

Rahn, Esposito, Whitters and Barkla

Chris Rahn illustrates the original #107, its stamped promos and Phyrexian-language DMU #369. Joe Esposito illustrates English stained-glass showcase #290 and textured counterpart #331. The language and frame presentation should be checked from the exact record rather than inferred from the name.

Richard Whitters illustrates later borderless concept #435 and step-and-compleat #436. Their catalogue release is 3 February 2023 even though they retain DMU set codes: they are later set-extension treatments, not evidence that every DMU printing debuted that day. Nicky Barkla’s SDS #11 provides the Stardates art family.

Textured foil is not ordinary showcase foil

DMU #290 offers nonfoil and traditional foil; #331 is textured foil only. Concept #435 offers nonfoil/traditional foil, whereas #436 is step-and-compleat foil only. Those pairs must not be collapsed into a single record or an unrestricted finish selector.

The prerelease PDMU #107s is datestamped and foil-only. Promo-pack #107p offers nonfoil and traditional foil. SDS #11 also offers nonfoil/traditional foil; it is not thereby a textured, signed or serialized card. Compare DMU #107 nonfoil with SDS #11 nonfoil to isolate the artwork change, then compare #290 and #331 for the showcase surface.

Same draw-and-life engine across the editions

This {2}{B}{B} legendary Phyrexian Praetor has deathtouch. Your draws gain you 2 life; an opponent’s draws make that opponent lose 2 life. A crossover frame changes presentation, not those abilities or blanket format legality.

Use set, collector number, artist and finish to choose an edition. Promo stamps, Phyrexian script, texture and crossover imagery solve different collecting preferences. A scan cannot authenticate an expensive copy or prove its surface; use seller photographs and independent expertise where appropriate.
