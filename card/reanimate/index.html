Reanimate

Reanimate debuted in Tempest on 14 October 1997. TMP #151 is a Nonfoil uncommon illustrated by Robert Bliss. The same artwork returned in later paper foils. Alternate illustrations and treatments can be identified by the set and collector number.

Identifying your copy

Follow the artwork across releases

Robert Bliss’s Tempest artwork shows up again on the 2004 Friday Night Magic foil and the later Graveborn foil. The set mark and collector line identify the release when the illustration matches. Ultimate Masters #110 and Box Topper U12 instead share Johann Bodin’s artwork. Look for the extended-art frame and U12 when seeking the Box Topper.

Identify the alternate frames

Breaking News #18 is Michal Ivan’s newspaper-style showcase. FIC #282 is Wei Guan’s Final Fantasy Surge Foil. Grave Researcher // Reanimate appears in broad catalogue searches under its prepared spell name and has a separate Oracle identity.

A deck-building distinction

Reanimate returns a creature card from a graveyard and costs life equal to that card’s mana value. The catalogue lists Reanimate as Commander-legal and outside the Modern card pool on the review date. If you’re comparing it with other black staples that spend life or set up a later play, see Necropotence and Demonic Tutor.
