PRINTING HISTORY

Ancient Tomb across Magic's history

◇

Ancient Tomb first appeared in Tempest in 1997. Its ability to produce two colourless mana at the cost of life made it one of Magic's most recognisable acceleration lands.

Over the years, Ancient Tomb has returned through additional releases and special treatments, giving players and collectors a wider range of versions to choose from.

Those printings can vary considerably in artwork, frame style, finish and presentation, making Ancient Tomb particularly interesting to compare across sets.

VERSIONS & TREATMENTS

What changes between Ancient Tomb printings?

◇

Every Ancient Tomb performs the same fundamental job, but the physical versions can look very different from one another.

Depending on the release, you may find different illustrations, frames, set symbols, premium finishes and alternative treatments.

For a card that regularly appears in powerful decks, that creates a useful choice between an affordable gameplay copy and a more distinctive version for a favourite deck or collection.

WHICH ONE?

Which Ancient Tomb printing should you choose?

◇

For playing: compare the more affordable available versions first. If Ancient Tomb is going straight into a deck, the cheapest clean printing may be all you need.

For artwork: look through the available images and choose the frame and illustration that best suits the style of your deck.

For collecting: premium finishes and unusual treatments can make certain releases much more visually distinctive than a standard printing.
