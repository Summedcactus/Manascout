PRINTING HISTORY

Teferi's Protection across its releases

◇

Teferi's Protection first appeared in Commander 2017. Its unusual combination of phasing out permanents, protecting life total and removing its controller from many forms of interaction quickly made it one of white's most recognisable Commander protection spells.

Since then, the card has appeared in additional releases with different artwork, frames and premium treatments.

That relatively focused printing history makes the available versions easy to compare while still offering meaningful visual differences.

VERSIONS & TREATMENTS

What changes between Teferi's Protection printings?

◇

Teferi's Protection printings can differ through artwork, frame treatment, set identity, foil finish and other premium presentation.

Because the card is modern enough to have received collector-focused treatments, newer versions can feel dramatically different from the original Commander 2017 copy.

For a card closely associated with one of Magic's most recognisable planeswalkers, artwork can also play a major role in which printing feels right for a deck.
