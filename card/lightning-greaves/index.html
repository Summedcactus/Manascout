PRINTING HISTORY

Lightning Greaves across its releases

◇

Lightning Greaves first appeared in Mirrodin in 2003. Its combination of haste, shroud and zero equip cost quickly made it one of Magic's most recognisable pieces of Equipment.

Since then, Lightning Greaves has been reprinted across many products and eras of Magic, giving it a wide range of frames, artwork and finishes.

That long reprint history makes it a strong example of a card where the gameplay remains familiar while the visual identity changes considerably from one printing to another.

VERSIONS & TREATMENTS

What changes between Lightning Greaves printings?

◇

Lightning Greaves printings can differ through artwork, frame style, set identity, foil availability and special treatments while keeping the same core Equipment card.

Older versions reflect the visual style of earlier Magic, while newer releases can offer updated artwork, cleaner frames or premium presentation.

Because Lightning Greaves appears in so many decks, choosing a version can be a good way to give an otherwise familiar staple more personality.

WHICH ONE?

Which Lightning Greaves printing should you choose?

◇

For a deck copy: compare the lower-priced available printings first if your priority is simply adding Lightning Greaves to a deck.

For classic Magic: older-frame versions may be more appealing if you prefer the look and feel of the card's earlier releases.

For collecting: foil finishes and more unusual treatments can make certain versions feel much more distinctive than a standard reprint.
