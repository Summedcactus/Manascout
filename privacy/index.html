Privacy Policy

Last updated: 7 September 2026

What ManaScout does

ManaScout does not require an account and this version does not intentionally collect names, passwords or payment details.


Search requests

When you search for a card, your browser requests card information from the Scryfall API. Scryfall may process technical request information under its own policies.


Hosting

This site is hosted using GitHub Pages. GitHub may process technical information such as IP addresses and request logs for security, reliability and service operation.


Cookies and analytics

This initial version does not intentionally set advertising or analytics cookies. If analytics, advertising or affiliate services are introduced later, this policy will be updated as appropriate.


External links

ManaScout links to third-party services such as Scryfall, Cardmarket and TCGplayer. Their own privacy policies apply when you visit them.

ManaScout is an independent MTG discovery tool.
